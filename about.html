Our Academic Mission

To deliver highly structured, premium linguistic education that bridges physical gaps and supports scholars to realize their global scholastic and professional potential.

Our Global Vision

To establish the most advanced, engaging, and personalized international language training ecosystem where modern tech meets verified expert pedagogy.

Our Modern Pedagogy

Moving beyond passive reading. We emphasize conversational immersion, live peer sprints, native speaker pods, and constant audio accent feedbacks.

Our Legacy

Milestones & Development

Trace our academy's history from a simple local language center to an international EdTech academy leader.

2021

The Inception

Fluentra was founded by a passionate advisory group of Oxford and Sorbonne language scholars with 10 native tutors instructing French and German.

2023

Scaling to 10k Scholars

Launched our online portals, expanded language offerings to Japanese and Korean, and partnered with international corporate recruitment networks.

2025

Linguistic EdTech Leader

Recognized as the Academy of the Year, integrating interactive peer speech chatrooms and launching Goethe & IELTS pre-tests channels.
