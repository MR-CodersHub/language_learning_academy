Frequently Asked Questions

Home FAQ

Are the academy tutors native speakers?

Yes, 100% of our core tutors are certified native speakers with extensive university linguistics training and professional teaching experience across international exam boards.

Do you guarantee passing Goethe and IELTS examinations?

Our Fluency Pro and Elite tracks contain dedicated prep drills, extensive oral exercises, and mock examinations that mirror official structures. Scholars who complete our recommended modules maintain a 99% first-try success rate.

How do the live conversational chatrooms work?

Conversational chatrooms are active 24/7. They are divided into proficiency tracks (A1, B1, etc.) and managed by native mentors who moderate dialogues, introduce fun vocabulary games, and evaluate active spoken pronunciations.

Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my plan at any time?

Absolutely. Fluentra features complete contract flexibility. You can modify, upgrade, or cancel your subscription plan directly from your dashboard in one click with zero penalty fees.

Do I get official graduation certificates upon completion?

Yes. Upon passing the module-end writing audits and oral accent checks, we issue a secure, certified Fluentra Language Graduation Certificate outlining your achieved CEFR level (A1 to C2).
